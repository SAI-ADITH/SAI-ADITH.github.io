## About Me

I'm **Sai Adith Senthil Kumar**, an **AI researcher and engineer** at **George Mason University**. I graduated with my **B.S. in Computer Science in May 2026** and am continuing into the **M.S. in Computer Science** program with a concentration in **Machine Learning**.

I'm interested in understanding how language models work and making them safe and reliable. I've worked on both research and production AI systems, with a focus on accuracy, latency, and reliability.

Outside of work, I've been playing **tennis** since 1st grade and used to play professionally. I also like going to the gym and listening to music.

## Research Interests

- Mechanistic interpretability (circuits, Sparse Autoencoders, activation patching, feature analysis, causal interventions)
- Evaluating interpretability methods (circuit minimality, faithfulness, how well explanations predict model errors)
- Post-training for interpretability (making model behaviors easier to explain and verify)
- AI safety and alignment (instruction-following reliability, constraint satisfaction, robustness testing)
- Persona simulation and counterfactual behavior in LLMs (persona-conditioned performance, steering)
- Reasoning behavior in LLMs ("thinking" vs. "no-thinking", trace relevance, failure modes under constraints)
- Deployment-aware safety evaluation (monitoring signals, hallucination reduction, RAG evaluation)
