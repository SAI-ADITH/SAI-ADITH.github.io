## About Me

I'm **Sai Adith Senthil Kumar**, a **Computer Science** researcher and engineer at **George Mason University**. I graduated with my **B.S. in Computer Science in May 2026** and am continuing into the **M.S. in Computer Science** program with a concentration in **Machine Learning**.

My work focuses on **LLM interpretability and instruction-following**, with an emphasis on **activation-level methods** (sparse autoencoders, activation patching), **persona simulation**, and **reasoning efficiency** ("thinking" vs. "no-thinking" behavior under strict constraints).

In industry, I've built and deployed end-to-end AI systems including **multi-agent LangGraph workflows**, **OCR + RAG pipelines**, and **real-time insight generation** with a focus on measurable improvements in **accuracy, latency, hallucination reduction, and reliability**.
